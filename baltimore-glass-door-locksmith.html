Baltimore Neighborhoods & Street Access

Use these neighborhood and street references to describe your location when you call or message on WhatsApp. Confirm service availability, timing, and the exact property address before arranging access.

Downtown, Inner Harbor & Mount Vernon

For Pratt Street, Light Street, and Charles Street properties, give the building name, loading entrance, and security contact. Identify whether glass fronts a public sidewalk, a lobby, or an interior tenant space.

Fells Point, Canton & Federal Hill

Near Broadway and Thames Street in Fells Point, O'Donnell Street in Canton, or South Charles Street in Federal Hill, mention narrow approaches, steps, outdoor seating, and any permitted rear access. Keep customers away from broken street-facing panes.

Hampden & Charles Village

For West 36th Street, St. Paul Street, and nearby residential or retail addresses, explain whether the opening is in a rowhouse, shop, or managed building. Baltimore City and Baltimore County are different service locations; confirm the full address and availability when calling.

For exposed or unstable glass, keep people clear of the opening. Call 202-643-4062 to discuss urgent damage. For messaging, use WhatsApp at 703-244-0559.
