Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for business owners and property managers looking for commercial glass repair, storefront glass replacement, door hardware service, and emergency board-up.

Emergency & Response

Do you offer 24/7 emergency commercial glass repair?

The phone line accepts emergency commercial glass calls 24/7. Call 703-244-0559 with the service location, condition of the opening, and access details so next steps can be discussed.

What happens first when glass is broken?

Keep people away from damaged glass. Call with the service location and opening details so temporary secure-up and replacement options can be discussed.

How is arrival timing discussed?

Timing depends on the location, access, opening condition, and current scheduling. Call with those details so available next steps can be discussed.

Services & Scope

Do you work with property managers and multi-location businesses?

Property managers and multi-location businesses can call to discuss the location, scope, access requirements, and scheduling for each property.

Can you repair commercial door hardware like closers and panic bars?

Call to discuss issues involving commercial door closers, hinges, pivots, or panic hardware. Service scope depends on the specific door and hardware condition.

What types of glass do you work with?

Commercial jobs often involve tempered, laminated, and insulated glass. The correct type depends on the opening and project conditions. Call to discuss the opening and service location.

Calling & Scheduling

What details should I provide when I call about commercial glass work?

Provide the service location, a description of the opening, any known measurements, and access details. Some projects may require an onsite assessment before the scope is confirmed.

Can you work around business hours?

Scheduling depends on access requirements and the type of work. Call with your constraints so available scheduling options can be discussed.

How do I discuss scheduled work?

Call 703-244-0559 and describe the opening, damage, service location, and access conditions.
