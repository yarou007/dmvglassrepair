Emergency Board-Up Process

Secure the opening → assess and measure → coordinate glass replacement.

1. Secure the Opening

The first task is temporary protection for the damaged storefront, window, door, or other exposed opening.

2. Assess and Measure

The damaged area, surrounding frame, glass type, and exact dimensions are reviewed for the permanent repair.

3. Coordinate Glass Replacement

Once the required glass and measurements are known, the permanent replacement and installation can be coordinated.

What Happens After the Call

We review the opening and access details you provide, identify what needs to be assessed on site, and discuss board-up as the temporary step when the property cannot remain exposed. The on-site assessment determines the secure-up approach and supplies the measurements needed for replacement planning.

  • Access and damaged-opening details are reviewed by phone
  • The opening and remaining glass are assessed on site
  • Temporary board-up is completed when appropriate
  • Replacement glass is identified and coordinated separately

Commercial Storefronts, Offices, Doors, and Windows

Emergency board-up is used for broken retail storefronts, restaurant entrances, office windows, glass doors, apartment and managed-property openings, and other damaged areas that cannot be left unsecured.

Storefront Systems

Large panes, door glass, sidelites, and transoms connected to a commercial entrance.

Office and Managed Properties

Windows and common-area entrances where site access or tenant use affects the secure-up plan.

Break-In and Impact Openings

Damaged doors and windows after forced entry, vandalism, storms, or accidental impact.

Board-Up Is Temporary Protection

Board-up does not replace the permanent glass repair. Custom dimensions, glass thickness, safety treatment, and frame conditions must be confirmed before the correct replacement can be prepared. The board-up keeps the immediate secure-up task separate from that permanent work.
