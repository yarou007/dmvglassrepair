What to Do When Your Storefront Glass Breaks in Washington DC

A broken storefront can disrupt sales, expose inventory, and create a safety risk. This guide explains the first steps business owners should take before repair crews arrive.

What to Do in the First Few Minutes

  1. Move customers and staff away from the damaged pane and block access from both sides.
  2. Do not touch loose shards, lean material against the opening, or attempt unsafe measurements.
  3. Check whether the storefront can still be locked without approaching broken glass.
  4. Record the time, cause, affected opening, and visible damage for the property or insurance file.
  5. Call and explain whether the opening is cracked, shattered, missing glass, or unsecured.

Information to Give During the Call

Start with the Washington DC address and whether the damaged opening is a fixed display pane, entry-door glass, sidelite, or transom. Explain whether the building is occupied, whether the door still closes, and how service personnel can access the site. Approximate dimensions and visible glass markings are useful only when they can be checked safely.

When Board-Up Comes Before Replacement

A cracked pane that remains contained may be handled differently from a missing or shattered panel. If the opening cannot be safely completed during the first visit, emergency board-up can provide temporary secure-up while the storefront is measured and the correct glass is prepared.

Secure the Opening

The immediate priority is limiting access to sharp glass and reducing exposure when the storefront no longer closes or locks.

Assess and Measure

Fixed panes, doors, sidelites, and transoms are separate openings. Each must be measured and matched to the existing system.

Plan Permanent Replacement

The glass type, dimensions, frame condition, entry operation, and site access determine the permanent replacement scope.

Coordinate the Business Follow-Up

Tell staff which entrance is unavailable, identify the person authorized to approve work, and keep incident and service notes together. If the damage also affects the aluminum entrance, closer, pivot, or lock alignment, note that during the call so the glass and commercial door problem can be separated into clear scopes.

Final Takeaway

Keep people away from the glass, determine whether the opening is secure, and call with the exact pane location and access details. The Washington DC storefront glass repair page explains what happens from dispatch through permanent replacement.
