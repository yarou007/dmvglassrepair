Storefront, Door, Office, and Interior Glass

Storefront and Door Glass

Fixed storefront panes, entry-door glass, sidelites, and transoms are measured as separate openings. Related frame or door-operation problems are identified when they affect the glass.

Office Windows and Insulated Units

Cracked panes, impact damage, and failed insulated glass units require measurements that account for the existing window or glazing system.

Applicable Interior Glass

Interior office panels and partitions can be assessed when the glass type, mounting system, site access, and work-area conditions are suitable for service.

Safety Glazing

Tempered or laminated glass may be required based on the opening and existing system. Markings, location, dimensions, and use are reviewed before replacement.

Property Manager and Multi-Site Workflow

A consistent phone-first intake helps building teams separate urgent openings from scheduled work and keep each location clear.

1. Identify the Property

Provide the address, suite or tenant, building contact, damaged opening, and whether the area is currently secure.

2. Confirm Access

Share loading, elevator, parking, security-desk, occupied-space, or after-hours access requirements that affect the visit.

3. Define the Scope

Each opening can be documented by location, glass condition, measurements, related frame conditions, and the proposed next step.

4. Coordinate Approvals

Estimate, contact, and scheduling information can be organized to support owner approval, tenant coordination, and multi-site tracking.

What to Provide During the Phone Call

  • The Washington DC property address, business type, and onsite contact.
  • The opening location: storefront, entry door, office window, lobby, or interior area.
  • Whether the glass is cracked, shattered, missing, fogged, or leaving an opening unsecured.
  • Approximate dimensions and visible glass markings only when they can be obtained safely.
  • Access limits, tenant hours, approval steps, purchase-order needs, or documentation requirements.
  • For multiple sites, a separate address and contact for each property.

After the call, the opening can be assessed, secured if necessary, measured, and matched to the appropriate replacement. Permanent installation is coordinated after the required glass and site conditions are confirmed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between emergency and scheduled commercial glass service?

Emergency service focuses on broken glass, unsafe conditions, and unsecured openings, with board-up when needed. Scheduled service is appropriate for planned replacement, failed insulated units, interior glass, and other work that can be coordinated around building access.

Can you coordinate commercial glass work for multiple properties?

Yes. Property managers and multi-site operators can identify each location, opening, access contact, and approval requirement by phone so scopes and scheduling can be organized by property.

What types of commercial glass do you handle?

Service can include storefront panes, commercial door glass, office windows, insulated glass units, and applicable interior glass such as partitions. The opening is assessed to identify the correct glass and scope.

What should a property manager provide during the phone call?

Provide the property address, building and tenant contact, type and location of the damaged glass, whether the opening is secure, access restrictions, approximate measurements if safely available, and any documentation or approval requirements.
