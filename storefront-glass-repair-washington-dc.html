Broken Storefront Panes, Door Glass, Sidelites, and Transoms

A damaged storefront is not always one simple pane. The affected opening may include fixed display glass, entry-door glass, narrow sidelites beside the door, or a transom above it. Identifying the exact opening during the first call helps the right service be dispatched.

Cracked or Shattered Panes

Cracked glass should be assessed before the damage spreads. A shattered panel may also leave sharp edges or an exposed opening that needs immediate secure-up.

Entry-Door Glass

Door glass must be matched to the opening and checked alongside the surrounding frame so the entrance can operate safely after replacement.

Sidelites and Transoms

Damage beside or above an entry can involve separate glass sizes and framing conditions. Each opening is assessed and measured individually.

Tempered, Laminated, and Insulated Storefront Glass

Broken glass is normally replaced rather than patched. The existing system, pane location, dimensions, markings, and use of the opening help determine the appropriate replacement. A usable frame or related door hardware may be repairable even when the glass itself must be replaced.

Tempered Safety Glass

Tempered glass is commonly used in doors and other high-contact locations. Existing stamps and the position of the pane help identify what is required.

Laminated Safety Glass

Laminated glass is made to remain held together after breakage. The existing glazing and storefront requirements are reviewed before ordering replacement.

Insulated Glass Units

Some storefront and commercial window systems use sealed multi-pane units. Fogging, seal failure, cracks, and impact damage require accurate unit measurements.

What to Tell Us When You Call

You do not need a technical diagnosis before calling. A few practical details help Capital Glass Door understand the opening and plan the next step:

  • The Washington DC service address and business or building type.
  • Whether the damage is in a fixed pane, entry door, sidelite, or transom.
  • Whether glass is cracked, shattered, missing, or leaving the property unsecured.
  • Approximate width and height only if they can be obtained without approaching broken glass.
  • Any visible tempered-glass stamp or other glass marking.
  • Site-access details, including occupied spaces, locked entrances, or building contacts.

Do not handle loose glass or attempt unsafe measurements. The opening can be assessed and measured during service.

From Dispatch to Board-Up and Permanent Replacement

1. Call and Dispatch

Describe the location, damaged opening, current safety concern, and site access so the service need can be reviewed.

2. Secure and Assess

Loose or exposed areas are assessed. If permanent replacement cannot be completed immediately, board-up may be used to secure the opening.

3. Measure and Match

The pane, frame, glass type, and surrounding storefront conditions are measured so the permanent replacement can be prepared.

4. Replace and Check

The replacement glass is installed, the repaired area is checked, and entry-door operation is reviewed when the damaged opening is part of the entrance.

A cracked or shattered pane generally requires replacement. Separate problems with a reusable aluminum frame, closer, pivot, or alignment may be handled as related storefront or commercial door repair.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you respond to broken storefront glass after hours?

Yes. Capital Glass Door provides 24/7 emergency response for broken storefront glass. When replacement cannot be completed immediately, board-up may be used to secure the opening.

What information should I have when I call?

Share the Washington DC service address, where the damaged pane is located, whether it is a door, sidelite, transom, or fixed panel, whether the opening is secure, and any approximate measurements or glass markings you can obtain safely.

Do you replace tempered and laminated storefront glass?
