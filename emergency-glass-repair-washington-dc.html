When the Opening Needs to Be Secured

Permanent replacement is not always possible during the first visit because the glass type, thickness, safety treatment, and exact dimensions must match the opening. When the property cannot be left exposed, emergency board-up can provide temporary protection while replacement glass is coordinated.

  • Storefront damage after a break-in, vandalism, or impact
  • Shattered door glass, sidelites, or transoms at an entrance
  • Broken windows facing a sidewalk, parking area, or shared space
  • Loose glass or an opening that cannot be isolated safely

What to Tell Us When You Call

A clear phone description helps identify whether the first priority is securing the opening, measuring for replacement, or addressing both.

  • The property location and whether it is a storefront, office, managed building, or home
  • Whether the damaged opening is a pane, entry door, sidelite, transom, or window
  • The approximate width and height, only if they can be checked safely
  • Whether loose glass remains or the interior is exposed
  • Access instructions and any restrictions around the damaged area

What Happens After the Call

1. Review the Opening

We use the details you provide to understand the damaged opening, access conditions, and whether temporary secure-up may be needed.

The opening is assessed on site, secured when necessary, and measured so the required replacement can be identified.

3. Coordinate Replacement

Once the glass specification and dimensions are confirmed, the permanent replacement and installation can be coordinated.
