Frequently Asked Questions

What determines storefront glass replacement timing?

Timing depends on glass type, panel size, site access, and the condition of the opening. Scheduling is discussed by phone after those details are reviewed.

What usually causes delays?

Custom fabrication, specialty glass requirements, hardware coordination, and restricted site access windows can extend lead time.

Can you board-up first and replace later?

Temporary secure-up and later replacement may be separate steps, depending on the opening and site conditions. The sequence is confirmed by phone.
