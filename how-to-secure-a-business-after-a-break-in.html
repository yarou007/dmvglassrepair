How to Secure a Business After a Break-In

After a break-in, the right first steps protect people, preserve evidence, and reduce revenue loss. This checklist helps owners and managers act quickly and safely.

Call 202-643-4062 WhatsApp

Step 1: Protect People and Preserve Evidence

Keep staff and customers away from the damaged zone, contact authorities, and document the scene before cleanup. Once approved, secure the opening with emergency board-up.

Need to discuss an exposed opening? Call 202-643-4062. The emergency call line is answered 24/7.

Step 2: Secure the Building Envelope

Replace or reinforce vulnerable openings, check lock function, and verify door closer performance. If replacement glass is not immediately available, maintain secure temporary protection.

  • Board-up exposed openings and isolate broken glass zones
  • Check lock cylinders, strike plates, and panic hardware function
  • Coordinate replacement timeline with staff and landlord teams

Step 3: Plan Reopening and Permanent Repairs

Call with the incident timing, condition of the opening, property access notes, and onsite contact so the repair scope and next steps can be discussed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do first after a break-in?

Prioritize people and follow directions from the relevant authorities. Once the site can be accessed, call to discuss the exposed opening and temporary secure-up options.

When might temporary board-up be considered?

When an opening remains exposed, temporary secure-up may be one option before permanent work. The appropriate scope depends on the site conditions and access.

What information should I provide when I call?

Provide the exact address, the condition of the opening, access notes, and contact details for the person onsite.

Break-In Recovery Takeaway

Secure-up first, then restore properly. A structured response helps businesses reopen faster and lowers repeat risk after a break-in event.
