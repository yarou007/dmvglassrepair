Tempered Glass & Safety Standards

Shower doors require safety glazing. We follow ANSI Z97.1 and CPSC 16 CFR 1201 guidance and use tempered glass with proper thickness and hardware load ratings.

Learn more about glass safety standards on our Safety Compliance page.
